## Full Description (for AMO listing page)

### Weather & Clock Dashboard

Transform your Firefox new tab into a sleek, information-rich dashboard.

**Features:**
- **Live Weather** — Current conditions and 3-day forecast powered by wttr.in. Set any city worldwide.
- **World Clocks** — Track time across multiple time zones. Add, remove, and customize your clock list.
- **Search Bar** — Clean, prominent search bar powered by Yahoo.
- **Dark & Light Mode** — Toggle between themes with one click. Your preference is remembered.
- **Fast & Lightweight** — Pure HTML/CSS/JS, no frameworks. Loads instantly on every new tab.
- **Privacy-Friendly** — No personal data collection. Anonymous usage analytics only. No tracking cookies.

**How to use:**
1. Install the extension — your new tab is automatically replaced
2. Click the pin icon on the weather widget to set your city
3. Use the dropdown in the World Clocks section to add time zones
4. Toggle dark/light mode with the sun/moon button in the top right

**Permissions explained:**
- `storage` — Save your preferences (theme, city, time zones) locally
